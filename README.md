# Sky-Felicia

**Design, build, and simulate space missions.** Sky-Felicia is an educational space mission design game where players balance spacecraft engineering, orbital constraints, scientific goals, and limited resources.

## Why this game

Space engineering is often taught as theory or in tools that focus on just one part of a mission. Sky-Felicia aims to make the connections between mission planning, spacecraft design, operations, and science approachable through interactive decisions and clear explanations.

The game is being designed for students and hackathon participants with a range of space-science experience, including beginners.

## Planned highlights

- Design a spacecraft and choose instruments, launch vehicles, and orbits.
- Make tradeoffs among mass, power, cost, communications, lifetime, and scientific value.
- Operate missions, respond to unexpected events, and manage spacecraft health.
- Learn through concise explanations, optional deeper material, and performance feedback.
- Work together in teams of up to four players.

These are design goals, not a claim that every feature is already implemented.
