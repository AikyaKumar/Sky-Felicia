# Orbital Frontier — First expedition

Open `Orbital_Frontier_Expedition.html` in desktop Chrome or Edge. In Unity, open `Assets/OrbitalFrontier/Scenes/Expedition.unity` and press Play.

1. Open **First-flight guide** from Mission Design and choose **Fly the guided Moon mission**. The guide loads a working ship and shows where to go next. To build your own ship, open **Shipyard**, click a part on the 3D ship, and try a different component in its mount. Check the launch requirements before committing.
2. Walk to **Helm** and separate both stages when prompted. At **Navigation**, accelerate coast. If power fails, use **Engineering** to run diagnostics and replace the controller. Return to **Navigation** for orbital capture.
3. At **Science**, measure the landing corridor. Return to **Helm** and begin descent. Guided mode starts with computer-controlled thrust. Expert gives you throttle, lateral thrusters and attitude dampers.
4. After touchdown, take the aft **Airlock** outside. Open the surface map, follow the range and bearing to A and B, and collect each field record within 10 m. If you drive the rover, dismount to take the measurements. Watch suit oxygen and power on the return to the hatch.
5. Back in the cabin, open **Communications**, transmit the data, and check your score when Earth acknowledges the report.

| Input | Action |
|---|---|
| WASD or arrow keys | Move on the ship or surface |
| Click the viewport, then mouse | Look around. Esc releases the pointer. |
| E | Operate a reachable console, use a hatch or measure a field site |
| M / V | Open the map / cycle first-person, exterior and map views |
| R | Mount or dismount an installed rover within range |
| [ / ] | Decrease or increase throttle during manual descent |

Select the **?** beside a term, resource, component, or design hold to see what it means and how the simulation uses it. Where useful, the explanation links to the NASA, JPL, or terrain-data source.

The deck map tracks your position and the live guide shows the distance to the next console. You can open a console from the directory when it says **In reach**. For Jezero, choose **Mars** in Mission Design and use the **Guided** flight profile for a first landing.

For a crew of up to four, see the [Windows browser crew instructions](Docs/CREW_HOST.md) or the native Unity LAN instructions in the [project README](README.md). For the earlier orbital reconnaissance scenario in `MissionDesk.unity`, see the [survey quickstart](Docs/SURVEY_QUICKSTART.md).
