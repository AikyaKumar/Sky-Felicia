

Functional Requirements:
1- Mission:
Design and plan Space mission: define mission objective and requirements (Scientific Objective) - make custom missions possible
Manage Space mission: Complete Mission Objective - without score
Simulate the mission
Make informed decisions on any sort of failure - should add base 
2- Engineering: (Engineering Requirements)
Every major design decision has a consequence. For example, increasing the number of instruments could increase scientific capabilities but also increase mass, power consumption, cost, and data generation.
Spacecraft Design and configuration: structure, power systems, batteries, comm, computers, altitude control/ trajectory control, scientific instruments, health monitoring systems (monitors food). Properties: mass, power consumption, cost, data generation, scientific value.
Select and Use Scientific Instruments.
Launch Vehicle planning
Orbital selection: provide many options and their characteristics explanation.
Communications
Different levels of Space knowledge - half is there and the rest should be built
3- Collaboration:
Collaborate with a team of up to 4 people 
4- Mission Validation/Evaluation
Scoring systems
Provide feedback on mission performance and reward effective engineering decisions, efficient resource management, successful collaboration, scientific value,  and achievement of mission objectives. Explain why a decision affects mission performance rather than just fail or success. 
Allow users to refine their designs (failed design is saved) - add to base
Have multiple right combinations for the same problem or mission
5. Spacecraft Payload
Components Range (simple to advanced)  - add to base
Spacecraft compatibility for a specific orbit - mission lifetime, communication, costs, payload capacities. 
6. Levels
different difficulty levels, mission types, or scenarios.
More difficult means more advances spacecraft components.
7- Education
Consider including short explanations, tutorials, visual indicators, or optional learning resources. Fixed simple explanation but user can choose to learn more. 


Rules/Constraints:

Limited Budget, power, spacecraft mass (Available Resources)
Communications Capacity (Operational Constraints)
Orbital Constraints
Mission Lifetime
Random events/unexpected mission constraints. (eg astroids)
Goal: Complete mission with highest performance.


Target Audience:
Students who rarely get to experiment with an entire mission from concept to operation
accessible to participants with different levels of knowledge about space science and engineering.






Problems to solve: 
Space engineering concepts are mostly theoretical: so we are building a game that teaches students practical aspects 
Existing educational tools specifically focus on individual aspects of space missions: Our game should focus on mission planning, spacecraft design, resource management and health related aspects of astronaut and spacecraft 
Complex space science can discourage beginners: Our app should use visualization, simple explanation, and interactive gameplay




Possible Game references:
Trailmakers, No man sky, sonar submarine operators, space flight simulator
